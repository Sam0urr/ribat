A watchpost for geopolitical exposure. Not where risk originates — who bears it. The Intensity index weights country GPR by trade, energy, raw-material and chokepoint dependency. The sliders are your channel mix. What does this measure?

Map view

Exposure: every economy shaded by the risk it bears. Switch to Risk sources and the map inverts - it shades every economy by how much of the selected country's Intensity that economy produces.

Normalisation

Each source country indexed to its own 1985–2023 mean = 100. Detects deterioration; understates chronically high-risk states.

Weights renormalise to sum to 1. There is no correct fixed weighting — move them and read the result as a sensitivity analysis, not a ranking.

Trade weight basis

Gross: bilateral goods flows (Comtrade). Value added: origin of value added in final demand (OECD TiVA) — looks through third countries, so exposure routed via intermediaries is attributed to its true origin.
