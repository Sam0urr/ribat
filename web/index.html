Map view

Exposure: every economy shaded by the risk it bears. Switch to Risk sources and the map inverts - it shades every economy by how much of the selected country's Intensity that economy produces.

Normalisation

Each source country indexed to its own 1985–2023 mean = 100. Detects deterioration; understates chronically high-risk states.

Weights renormalise to sum to 1. There is no correct fixed weighting — move them and read the result as a sensitivity analysis, not a ranking. The default (55 / 25 / 10 / 10) is an illustrative starting point, not a derived weighting.

Trade weight basis

Gross: bilateral goods flows (Comtrade). Value added: origin of the value added embodied in this economy's final demand (OECD TiVA FDVA). It looks through third countries, but it measures what this economy's consumers and investors ultimately absorb, finished goods included — not what its producers use as inputs (METHODOLOGY §5.2).

Coverage

As observed: each channel is its covered share × the mean partner GPR over the partners GPR observes, so a thinly covered economy ranks low for a coverage reason, not an exposure one. Per observed dependency divides each channel by its covered share (chokepoint has no coverage measure and is unchanged); this removes the confound but amplifies noise where coverage is thin — energy coverage falls to 0.09 (METHODOLOGY §5.4).
